ESP32-S3 · LVGL 8.4 · BLE 5.0 HID · reveal.js

Controlador táctil circular con temporizador de escenario

Circular BLE HID touch presenter with built-in stage timer

Sustituye los pasadores de dos botones por una pantalla IPS capacitiva de 1.46" basada en Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.46. Se empareja como teclado Bluetooth estándar sin drivers e integra navegación 2D para reveal.js, retroalimentación acústica y carcasa 3D.

Replaces conventional two-button clickers with a 1.46" circular capacitive IPS touchscreen powered by Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.46. Pairs as a standard driverless Bluetooth HID keyboard with full 2D reveal.js navigation, acoustic haptic clicks, and a 3D-printable pebble shell.

2. Stage Control

  • ESC: Vista general (Overview).
  • b: Pantalla negra (Blackout).
  • HOME: Primera diapositiva.
  • f: Pantalla completa + ENTER central.
  • ESC: Overview mode.
  • b: Blackout screen.
  • HOME: Jump to first slide.

3. Cronómetro Local

3. Stage Timer

  • Reloj hardware esp_timer_get_time() activo en segundo plano.
  • +1 / +2 / +5 min en vivo, PLAY / PAUSA y STOP / RESET (mantener 1.5 s).
  • Aviso ámbar (< 2 min) y rojo parpadeante + alarma a 00:00.
  • Hardware esp_timer_get_time() countdown runs across all screens.
  • Live +1 / +2 / +5 min add, PLAY / PAUSE, 1.5 s hold-to-reset.
  • Amber (< 2 min) and blinking red + double-pulse chime at 00:00.

Audio Háptico y Energía

Haptic Audio & Power

DAC I2S PCM5101 sintetizando un clic mecánico amortiguado de 18 ms sin bloquear LVGL. Atenuación PWM al 15% tras 10 s sin toque y Deep Sleep tras 15 min sin BLE ni interacción (despertar por botón físico PWR en GPIO 6).

PCM5101 I2S DAC renders an 18 ms damped mechanical click off the UI thread. Backlight auto-dims to 15% PWM after 10 s idle and enters Deep Sleep after 15 min without BLE or touch (wake via physical PWR button on GPIO 6).
